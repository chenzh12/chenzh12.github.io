About

I work on supply chain optimization and order fulfillment at JD.com. My work combines demand forecasting, operations research, and machine learning to improve inventory allocation and fulfillment decisions.

I earned my bachelor's and doctoral degrees in the Department of Industrial Engineering at Tsinghua University. At the Institute of Operations Research and Statistics, my doctoral research examined how the introduction of high-speed rail relates to domestic air passenger and cargo traffic in China.

Professional Experience

Research Interests

  • E-commerce Supply Chains and Fulfillment

    I study assortment planning for forward distribution centers, inventory allocation, and order splitting, with a focus on trade-offs between inventory costs and fulfillment service levels.

  • Decision-Focused Learning

    I study how to train predictive models using downstream optimization objectives and evaluate predictions by the quality of the resulting decisions.

  • Transportation Systems Analysis

    I use panel data models to study the relationship between high-speed rail entry and air passenger and cargo traffic.
